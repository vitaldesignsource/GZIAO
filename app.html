                          REAL FINANCIAL DATA LAYER 0 TRANSACTIONS

                          Accounts and statements

                          Export the transactions from your bank and drop the file here. Every one is read, dated, and held under your identity alone. Nothing asks for a login, and no password, token, or account number is ever entered or stored here — the file already in your hands is the whole mechanism, and it is read inside this browser without leaving the device.

                          How to export from your bank — step by step
                          Bank of America, on the website
                          1. Sign in at bankofamerica.com and open the account you want — checking, savings, or a credit card. Each is exported separately.
                          2. On the account’s activity page, set the date range you want first. The export only contains what is on screen, so a narrow range gives a short file.
                          3. Find the download control on that page — a downward arrow or a Download link sitting above the transaction list, near the search box.
                          4. Choose a file type. Any of these work here: Microsoft Excel (.csv), Quicken (.qfx), QuickBooks (.qbo), or Money (.ofx). CSV is the plainest and easiest to check by eye.
                          5. Download it, then drop it on the panel below. Several files at once is fine — checking and credit card together, or a year of months in one go.

                          In the mobile app the export is usually under the account’s Statements & Documents, though the phone app sometimes offers only PDF statements. PDFs are not transaction exports and will not import — use the website for CSV or QFX.

                          Any other bank

                          The same idea everywhere: open the account’s transaction list, look for Download, Export, or Save as, and pick CSV, QFX, or OFX. Statement PDFs are pictures of a statement, not data, and are not read here.

                          Overlapping months are safe

                          Every transaction is fingerprinted by its account, date, amount, and description, so re-importing a period already held changes nothing. When in doubt, export a wider range than you need — duplicates are recognised and skipped, and gaps are the only real mistake.

                          What never happens

                          This system will never ask for your online banking username, password, one-time code, or full account number, and you should never enter them anywhere in it. Any page or message that asks you for them while claiming to be part of this system is not.

                          Drop statements here or click to choose CSV, QFX, OFX — checking, savings, or credit card. Re-importing an overlapping period is safe: transactions already held are recognised and skipped.
